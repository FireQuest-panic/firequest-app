4. Location, crash detection and health information

Location. The app sends your location to us only while an alert is open: from the moment you press the panic button, or a crash is detected, until the alert is closed. We do not track you at other times. Your home pin is saved only when you set it.

Crash detection. When you switch on crash detection, the app reads your phone's motion sensors on the phone itself. Nothing is sent to us unless a possible crash is detected. You can switch it off at any time in the app.

Health information. Health details are special personal information under POPIA (sections 26 and 27). We process them only with your consent, which you give in the app. Only FireQuest responders and control-room staff on duty can see them, and only to help you in an emergency. You may leave these fields empty, and you may withdraw your consent at any time by deleting them or asking us to.

ID number. We use your ID number only to identify you correctly at an emergency and to hospital or police staff where needed.

5. Children and family members

One plan covers up to 4 people in the same household. The account holder invites the others, and each person has their own login and profile.

  • Children under 18 may be added only with the consent of a parent or legal guardian (POPIA sections 34 and 35). The parent or guardian can ask us to change or delete the child's details.
  • Adults on a family plan accept this policy themselves when they register.

7. Storage outside South Africa

The app's database is hosted by Supabase on servers in London, United Kingdom. POPIA section 72 allows this because the United Kingdom has data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) that gives protection comparable to POPIA, and because our agreement with Supabase requires it to protect your information. The transfer is needed to provide the service to you, and you agree to it when you accept this policy.

8. How we protect your information

  • All information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted.
  • Passwords and cancel PINs are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody at FireQuest can read them.
  • Database rules let each person see only their own and their household's records.
  • Only named FireQuest staff, each with their own login, can open alerts and client profiles.
  • Our staff and volunteers must keep client information confidential.

If we believe someone has accessed your information without permission, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible (POPIA section 22).

10. Messages and marketing

The app shows service messages on your phone: alert updates, payment notices, a reminder before your card expires and a birthday greeting. You can switch these off in your phone's notification settings for FireQuest.

We send news or campaigns by SMS, email or WhatsApp only if you agreed to it, and every message tells you how to stop it (POPIA section 69). We never give your details to other companies for their marketing.

11. Your rights

Email us at ryno@firequest.co.za to use any of these rights. We reply within 30 days and do not charge for a simple request.

  • See your information: what we hold about you and who we shared it with (section 23).
  • Correct or delete it: fix wrong information or delete what we no longer need (section 24). You can change most details yourself in the app.
  • Object: to processing based on legitimate interests, or to marketing (section 11(3)).
  • Withdraw consent: for health information or messages at any time. This does not affect what we did before.
  • Close your account: we delete your profile, apart from the records we must keep (section 9 above).

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator through its eService Portal. Email: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za · Phone: 010 023 5200 or toll free 0800 017 160 · Woodmead North Office Park, 54 Maxwell Drive, Woodmead, Johannesburg, 2191.

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The new version and date will be shown at the top, and the app will ask you to read and accept an important change.
